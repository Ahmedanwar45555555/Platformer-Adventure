# Platformer Adventure

Platformer Adventure is a small 2D platform game made with Godot.

You play as a character trying to make his way through the level by moving across platforms, jumping over gaps, collecting coins, and avoiding enemies.

The player can move left and right and has a double jump, which makes it possible to reach higher platforms and get over bigger gaps. I also added a dash that can be used with Shift to move quickly and get out of dangerous situations.

There are slimes moving around the level. Touching a slime causes the player to lose one health point. The player starts with three health points, and the health is shown on the screen. After taking damage, there is a short cooldown before the player can take damage again.

If the player loses all of their health, the level restarts from the beginning. Falling into the game's death zones also causes the player to restart.

The game also has coins to collect, animations for the player, moving enemies, platforms, obstacles, and a simple HUD for the player's health.

## Controls

- Left Arrow / Right Arrow - Move
- Space - Jump / Double Jump
- Shift - Dash

The main idea of the game is to get through the level, collect as many coins as possible, and avoid the enemies and hazards along the way.

The project was originally based on a platformer tutorial, and I used it as a starting point while adding and changing gameplay systems myself.
