Platformer Adventure
Platformer Adventure is a small 2D platformer game made with Godot 4.

You play as a character trying to navigate through the level by moving across platforms, jumping over gaps, and avoiding hazards to reach the final goal.

Gameplay & Mechanics
Player Movement: The player can move left and right, double jump to reach higher platforms, and use a Dash mechanism to quickly escape dangerous situations.

Health & Damage System: The player starts with 3 health points displayed on the screen HUD. Taking damage gives a brief temporary immunity cooldown.

Game Loop:

Game Over / Reset: Losing all health points or falling into any Kill Zone immediately restarts the level.

Victory Condition: Reaching the end object (the Palm Tree / Win Zone) immediately completes the level and reloads the stage.

Controls
A / D or Left Arrow / Right Arrow – Move

Space – Jump / Double Jump

Shift – Dash

Project Info
The project was originally based on a 2D platformer tutorial and was further developed by customizing mechanics, script logic, collision layers, and game flow using Godot Engine and GDScript.
